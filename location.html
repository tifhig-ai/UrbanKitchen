FAQ

quick answers before you visit

Where is Urban Kitchen located?

Urban Kitchen is located at 425 S. North County, Suite D in Pleasant Grove, Utah.

What are Urban Kitchen's hours?

We serve brunch Monday-Friday 8:00 AM-2:00 PM and Saturday-Sunday 8:00 AM-3:00 PM. Dinner is available every night 5:00 PM-9:00 PM.

Does Urban Kitchen serve brunch?

Yes. We serve brunch, breakfast, lunch, coffee, skillets, sandwiches, soups, salads, and pastries.

Does Urban Kitchen serve dinner every night?

Yes. Dinner is served every night from 5:00 PM-9:00 PM in Pleasant Grove.

What is on Urban Kitchen's dinner menu?

The dinner menu includes small plates, prime filet mignon, braised beef short ribs, chicken supreme, porchetta, seafood risotto, fusilli pasta, tiramisu, and cheesecake.

Does Urban Kitchen serve steak for dinner?

Yes. We serve prime filet mignon and braised beef short ribs for dinner, along with chicken, seafood risotto, pasta, and small plates.

Is Urban Kitchen a fine dining restaurant in Pleasant Grove?

Urban Kitchen offers a fine dining-inspired dinner with polished service, elevated plates, green velvet booths, and crystal chandeliers in a relaxed, welcoming atmosphere.

How do I reserve a table for dinner?

Request a table through our reservations page or call 801-406-2010. Your reservation is confirmed after our team contacts you.

Can Urban Kitchen host business meetings and celebrations?

Yes. We welcome business meetings, family gatherings, birthday dinners, baby showers, and wedding celebrations during brunch or dinner. Include the occasion and party size in your reservation request so our team can help plan your table.
