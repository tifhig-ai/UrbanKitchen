Brunch and Dinner in Pleasant Grove

brunch by day, dinner every night

Start with breakfast, sweet or savory brunch, sandwiches, salads, fresh juice, coffee, and kids meals. Return for Croatian and Mediterranean inspired small plates, steaks, chicken, pork, seafood, pasta, and desserts at dinner.

Breakfast favorites

The Basic, Urban Benedict, Breakfast Burrito, Flatiron Steak & Eggs, Berry Mascarpone French Toast, and skillets anchor the brunch menu.

Fresh juice and specialty drinks

Choose fresh-squeezed orange juice, rotating fruit-and-orange blends, seasonal specialty coffee, zero-proof Red Bull mocktails, chai, matcha, tea, and hot chocolate.

Lunch and lighter plates

Sandwiches, soup, Burrata Salad, and Classic Caesar cover quick lunches and slower midday gatherings.

Dinner every night

Dinner features tuna tartare, salads, prime filet mignon, braised short rib, chicken, porchetta, seafood risotto, pasta, and desserts.
