Our Story

brunch by day, dinner every night

Urban Kitchen starts with brunch and keeps going into dinner. Come in for French toast, benedicts, and lunch favorites during the day. At night, the menu shifts to steak, chicken, seafood, and pasta.

The same velvet booths and crystal chandeliers work beautifully for business meetings, family gatherings, birthday dinners, baby showers, and wedding celebrations, whether you stop in for coffee or stay for dinner.
